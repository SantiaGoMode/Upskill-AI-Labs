Interactive by design

Learners test prompts inside the work.

This is not a video course with a chatbot attached. Learners inspect evidence, choose allowed sources, test a prompt, examine the model trace, and turn the result into a reviewed workplace artifact.

  • 01Work with realistic evidenceNorthwind provides conflicting, stale, incomplete, and restricted material.
  • 02See what the model didOutput, provider trace, tokens, latency, and estimated cost remain visible.
  • 03Improve through evaluationFeedback grades grounding, completeness, judgment, efficiency, and guardrails.
